Sweden route GPX

Gothenburg → Stockholm · ~555 km · BRouter cycling track

Download ZIP (best on iPhone)

iPhone:

  1. Tap Download ZIP above.
  2. Tap the ↓ icon in Safari’s address bar (or open Downloads).
  3. Tap the zip → Save to Files or share to Google Drive.
  4. Unzip in Files, then import the .gpx into Google My Maps on a computer.

Includes waypoints: Gothenburg, Alingsås, Ulricehamn, Jönköping, Gränna, Vadstena, Linköping, Norrköping, Nyköping, Stockholm.
